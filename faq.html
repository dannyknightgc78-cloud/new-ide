Frequently Asked

Your questions, answered plainly.

Everything you need to know about how Haven protects your family — privacy, devices, browsers, and plans. Can't find it? Tap Ask Haven in the corner.

How do I set up Haven from scratch?

Four steps — full walkthrough on the Setup Guide:

  1. Create your account — pick Single Child / Device, Family Haven, or Sovereign Gold on the pricing page and complete Stripe checkout (Apple Pay or card).
  2. Enrol a device — Portal Admin → Add a device → enter the code on the device you want to protect.
  3. Install protection — Haven Family browser extension from the downloads page; add Haven to the home screen on phones and tablets.
  4. Verify — confirm green Verified status in the Portal Admin Chain of Trust.
How do I install Haven on Mac, iPhone, or Android?

Mac (Chrome / Edge): Download the Mac bundle or Chrome zip from Downloads, unzip, double-click Install Haven.command, follow the wizard, click Confirm setup, then choose Free tier or paste your Haven license in extension Options for the Portal Admin.

Mac (Safari): Use the Safari Resources zip and haven-safari-build.command (Xcode required) — see README_INSTALL.md inside the zip.

iPhone & iPad: Open haven.dannygc.cloud in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Enrol devices from the Portal Admin.

Android: Download haven-family-android.apk (WebView launcher v0.1.0) → allow install from Chrome/Files once → open APK → Install. Or open Haven in Chrome → Install app / Add to Home screen. For the Chrome extension, transfer the unzipped chrome folder to the device and load unpacked in chrome://extensions.

What is haven-failsafe-keys.json and why do I need it?

During setup, Haven prompts you to download an encrypted haven-failsafe-keys.json file. Store it on an external drive or a admin-only device — not on the device being protected.

This is your household recovery key. Only you hold it; Haven never keeps a copy in the cloud.

Does Haven work on all browsers?

Yes. The Haven website and Portal Admin work on all current browsers — Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, and Apple Safari, including Safari on iPhone and iPad. It's a standards-based web app, so there's nothing extra to install just to view your dashboard.

On a household member's device, protection is provided by the Haven Family browser extension you install during setup. It runs locally — blocking ads and trackers, guarding your vault, and sending only integrity check-ins.

How do I add another device?

Open the Portal Admin, choose Add a device, and enter the signed enrolment code on the new device. How many devices you can add depends on your plan:

  • Single Child / Device — 1 device.
  • Family Haven — up to 5 devices.
  • Sovereign Gold — unlimited devices on hardware you self-host.

Need more room? Upgrade your plan any time from the Portal Admin or the pricing page.

Can Haven read my child's messages, photos, or browsing?

No — and it can't, by design. Haven is anti-spyware by architecture. It never ingests message content, photos, or browsing history. The extension runs on the device and confirms protections are working — it does not scan what anyone types or views.

We hold the proof, not your data. Your household's words and content stay on your hardware; Haven only records cryptographic evidence that protection is intact.

Where is my data stored, and who holds the keys?

Haven is local-first. Your data and signing keys stay on your hardware — they never leave your home network. Integrity records are hash-chained and tamper-evident: altering any past entry breaks the chain, so silent tampering is mathematically detectable.

What's the difference between the subscription and the Sovereign self-host plan?

The Single and Family plans are simple monthly subscriptions — billing periods end on the last day of each calendar month (UTC). The Sovereign Gold plan is €49.99/year and gives you a self-host licence to run Haven on hardware you own, with unlimited devices and total control over your keys and data. Auto-renew is on by default; turn it off anytime in extension Options or the Stripe customer portal.

See the setup guide for how each is installed.

Is my payment secure, and can I cancel?

Payments are handled entirely by Stripe on their secure checkout page. Haven never stores your card or Apple Pay details — we never see them. Apple Pay and cards are supported automatically at checkout. All paid plans are subscriptions — cancel or manage auto-renew anytime via extension Options or the Stripe customer portal.

Will my child know Haven is installed?

Yes — and that's intentional. Haven is a Digital Mentor, not a monitor. It makes the rules visible and the protection transparent, so the conversation is shared rather than covert. You see verified safety hours and healthy digital minutes, not a feed of your child's private content.

Do you offer plans for schools or institutions?

Yes. Haven offers custom institutional rates for schools, districts, and group deployments — bulk licensing, term-based configs, and sovereign admin controls. Use School & Institutional Inquiry on the pricing page or submit the contact form and choose School / institutional.
