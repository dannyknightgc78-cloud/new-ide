Privacy Policy — Haven Family Browser Extension

Effective date: 2 July 2026 · Operator: Danny Knight / Haven (haven.dannygc.cloud)

Haven Family is a sovereign infrastructure interface for your browser — a Visible Mentor that connects to your Haven Node (Haven cloud or your own self-hosted node). The Extension is local-first: protection settings, admin gate credentials, vault data, and block counters stay on your device. We do not read messages, page text, photos, DMs, keystrokes, clipboard contents, or full browsing history.

Single purpose

The Extension has one job: maintain an encrypted connection to your Haven Node so families can prove agreed protection rules are intact, reflect admin commands (including Internet pause), and show a calm on-page mentor badge. It is not a surveillance tool.

What stays on your device

  • Admin gate — master-password hash and optional admin PIN hash (never transmitted).
  • Vault — encrypted credential bundle and salt (local only; master password never leaves hardware).
  • Preferences — device label, badge setting, admin protection policy, popup allowlists.
  • Block counters — aggregate counts (ads blocked, scams deflected, etc.).
  • Session minutes — approximate minutes the browser has been open (for admin dashboard summaries).
  • Blocked-event log (local) — up to 100 recent entries with hostname, block kind, and a short snippet (≤200 characters) when a scam or popup guard fires. Stored locally; optional sync to your Haven Node if enrolled.

What is sent to your Haven Node

API traffic uses HTTPS to the endpoint you configure (default https://haven.dannygc.cloud; Sovereign deployments may point to your own node). The developer has no access to self-hosted nodes. Transmissions are limited to:

  • Device enrollment — stable device ID, install fingerprint, child/device label, platform, extension version, and license token you enter.
  • License validation — your ABXLIC1 token is verified against your Haven Node (cloud or sovereign).
  • Integrity check-ins — periodic signed heartbeats confirming the Extension is installed and healthy. These pings carry no URLs, page content, or browsing history.
  • Admin policy sync — protection rules you set in Portal Admin (adult filter, social limits, site exclusions).
  • Blocked-event log (optional) — hostname, block kind, truncated snippet, and device ID when a block event is synced.
  • Upgrade prompts (minimal) — if you click an in-extension upgrade CTA, a single cta_click event (source label only) may be sent to your configured API base. No page data.

What we do not collect

  • Message content, photos, DMs, or social posts
  • Full browsing history or a log of every URL you visit
  • Keystrokes, form fields, or clipboard contents
  • Microphone, camera, or GPS location
  • Third-party advertising profiles or cross-site tracking

Safety controls — Kill-Switch and Chain of Trust

Kill-Switch (Internet pause)

When an admin triggers the kill-switch in Haven Portal Admin, your Haven Node marks the enrolled device as locked. On the next integrity check-in, the Extension learns that state and, when Enforce internet pause is enabled (default), blocks new top-level navigations in this browser only — redirecting to a calm lock page. Trusted Haven domains and your configured site exclusions remain reachable. The kill-switch does not upload browsing activity; it only reflects a signed admin command already stored on your Haven Node.

Chain of Trust (chain-sealing)

Haven maintains an append-only integrity ledger on your Haven Node. Each successful device check-in is Ed25519-sealed into this chain. The Extension polls chain status (PASS / FAIL), head hash, and entry count — storing only those integrity witnesses locally. Chain-sealing is a tamper-evident audit trail of check-ins, not a record of pages visited or content read. If the chain drifts, the Extension shows “system drift” until the ledger is healthy again.

Local sovereign helper

During direct/sovereign install, the Extension may talk to a local helper at http://127.0.0.1:17888 on your machine only — for license pickup, master-password handoff, and update checks. This traffic never leaves your hardware.

Optional C.A.R.L. maturity moderation (off by default) may call a local agent at http://127.0.0.1:8795 when explicitly enabled. No cloud relay.

Encryption and third parties

All Haven API traffic uses HTTPS/WSS where applicable. We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers, or embed third-party analytics SDKs in the Extension. Stripe handles payments on the Haven website; the Extension does not process payment card data.

Permissions explained

  • storage — save enrollment and settings locally
  • alarms — schedule integrity heartbeats without a persistent background page
  • declarativeNetRequest — apply ad/scam blocks and optional Internet-pause navigation blocks
  • activeTab — show the Visible Mentor badge on the current page
  • notifications — alert users to protection state changes
  • Host access (http/https) — inject the on-page mentor badge and reach your Haven Node (no content exfiltration)

Children and families

Haven Family is intended for use by families with administrative consent. Admins configure and manage the Extension. We follow data-minimisation principles aligned with COPPA and GDPR design intent.

Data retention and deletion

Device records can be removed from Portal Admin by the account holder. Uninstalling the Extension removes local storage on that browser. Contact us to request server-side deletion of enrollment records tied to your account.

Policy updates

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted here and noted in the Haven dashboard. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
