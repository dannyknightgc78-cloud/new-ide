Setup Guide

Up and protected in four steps.

Haven is designed to set up in minutes — mentor, not monitor. The browser extension runs locally on your device; your settings and vault stay on your machine. Follow the walkthrough below, and use the assistant in the corner if you get stuck.

  1. Step 1 — Create your account & choose a plan

    Pick the plan that fits your family on the pricing page and complete secure checkout (Apple Pay or card, powered by Stripe — we never see your card details).

    • Single Child / Device — protect one device.
    • Family Haven — up to 5 devices across the household.
    • Sovereign Gold — €49.99/year to run Haven on hardware you own, with unlimited household devices.

  2. Step 2 — Enrol a household device

    Open the Portal Admin and choose Add a device. Haven generates a short enrolment code. On the device you want to protect, enter the code to link it to your household.

    Sanctuary grows with your family. Set each child's maturity band in the Portal Admin — Child, Teen, or Adult — so protection adapts as they grow, not just static blocklists.

  3. Step 3 — Install the Haven browser extension

    Install from Chrome Web Store — one click when our listing is live.

    Mac (recommended): Download the Mac bundle, unzip, and double-click Install Haven.command. Follow the native wizard → click Confirm setup. Chrome auto-installs Haven when possible; the wizard only walks you through Load unpacked if needed.

    Chrome safety notice: Chrome may show “This extension is not listed in the Chrome Web Store…” — click Keep extension. Haven is installed from your download until our public Chrome Web Store listing goes live. Owner-built and safe.

    After install, complete Haven’s 3-step welcome in the browser: master password → download haven-failsafe-keys.json → pin the gold shield (puzzle piece → Haven Family → pin).

    Sanctuary grows with your family. In the Portal Admin, pick a Growth Path maturity band per device — protection tightens for younger children and relaxes as they earn trust.

    Free tier: ad blocker and password vault work without a license. Paste your Haven license in extension Options only for the Portal Admin.

    Also add Haven to the home screen on iPhone, iPad, or Android — see the install guide.

    Open downloads Install guide

    Important: Store haven-failsafe-keys.json on an external drive or admin-only device. This encrypted recovery file is yours alone — Haven never keeps a copy.

  4. Step 4 — Verify protection is live

    In the Portal Admin, open the Chain of Trust — a calm record of integrity check-ins for your household. A green Verified status means protection is working.

    Use Emergency lockdown when you need an instant internet pause across enrolled devices. Your failsafe keys file is there if you ever need to recover access.

Why it's secure

Local-first. Encrypted. Yours.

  • Runs on your device — the extension works in your browser; browsing content is never uploaded for scanning.
  • Encrypted vault — passwords are locked with a master password only you know.
  • Failsafe backup keys — your recovery file stays in your hands, not in the cloud.
  • Integrity proofs — Haven uses Ed25519 signatures to confirm protections are intact, like a tamper-evident seal — not a surveillance feed.
