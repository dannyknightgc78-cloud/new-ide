Sovereign Privacy

We verify system health — not your family's conversations.

Legacy parental apps work by reading everything: messages, photos, locations, browsing. That isn't safety — it's surveillance, and it makes your child a target for whoever breaches the vendor next.

Haven is built the opposite way. It is anti-spyware by architecture. Haven never ingests message content. Instead, a hardened local Compliance Shield continuously checks that the protections on the device are intact and behaving, and notarizes that proof. Your child's words stay your child's.

  • No message reading. Conversations, photos, and DMs are never collected or transmitted.
  • Local-first. The Compliance Shield runs on the device; your data and signing keys stay on your hardware.
  • Health, not content. Haven proves the guardrails are working — without becoming a window into your child's private life.

Tamper-Proof by Design

If anything changes, you know instantly.

Every safety rule and every event is cryptographically notarized into an append-only, hash-chained ledger. Altering any past record breaks the chain — so silent tampering is mathematically detectable.
